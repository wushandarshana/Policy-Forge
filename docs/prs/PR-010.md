## Scope
- **Renewal and lapse (AC-07.1–07.8).**
  - **Renewal cycle:** a nightly scheduler plus an ADMIN job endpoint, idempotent per date. Each offer is priced on the
    rule version active at that point (AC-07.3: a v2 fixture renews at 7400.00 while term 1 keeps 6850.00 on v1).
  - **Pay:** the owner's pay request takes an empty body, charges the offered premium and moves the policy to RENEWED
    (G-3).
  - **Lapse:** a grace-period lapse per product, on termEnd + 16 (MOTOR and HOUSEHOLD) or termEnd + 31 (TERM_LIFE).
  - **Re-pricing:** a sum-insured change re-prices a pending offer (AC-07.7).
  - **Job date bound:** the admin job date is bounded to today−30..today unless a test-only setting lifts it (AC-07.8).
- **Cancellation (AC-08.1–08.6, AC-10.3).**
  - **Refund:** pro-rata, with cooling-off on the first term only, and a fee whose result floors at 0.00. AC-08.1 is
    6850.00 × 265 / 365 − 500.00 = 4473.29.
  - **Preview:** a refund preview before cancelling.
  - **Records and reason:** append-only refund records (V11), and a required reason from 4 values (G-4).
  - **No backdating:** cancellation dates must be today or later (AC-08.6).
  - **Lapsed policies:** cancelling a LAPSED policy gives 409, with no refund written (the AC-10.3 API half, carried over
    from Sprint 3).
- **Admin dashboard (AC-11.1–11.6).** Active policies by product, premium collected (paid term premiums plus signed
  adjustments minus refunds, all exact decimals), the renewal pipeline (60 days) and the lapse forecast. ADMIN only,
  and `asOf` is bounded.
- **Sample-policy seed.** Synthetic policies for customer1 and customer2, including one near renewal and one past
  expiry within grace. Dates are relative to the injected clock (G-2). The seed uses a package-private, input-free
  backdated issuance path, guarded by ArchUnit.
- **Security hardening (S2/S3 carry-overs).** No `WWW-Authenticate` on any 401, so browsers never cache Basic
  credentials. A fixed problem `instance` URN instead of the request path. One shared sanitiser for echoed keys (64
  characters from `[A-Za-z0-9_.-]`). A fixed 503 when the policy-number sequence is exhausted. A fixed container error
  page.
- **Frontend.** Renew and Cancel panels on Policy Detail, the Refunds tab, and the Admin Dashboard page (KPI tiles,
  per-product table, pipeline and forecast; one column under 640 px). Playwright journeys on desktop and mobile, with
  committed visual baselines.
- **Specs first:** `7d95f67` (G-3 to G-6 from the plan checkpoint; G-6 vetoed, so SG-1 is accepted) and `404b5e4` (the
  security review's fixes, decided by the user).

**Fixed in this PR** ([trace](../fix-loops/2026-11-04-s4-review-fixes.md)):
- **Backdated cancellation:** customers could backdate a cancellation into cooling-off and get a full refund
  (blocker). Fixed by AC-08.6.
- **Stale renewal offer:** a renewal offer stayed at its old price after a sum-insured change. Fixed by AC-07.7.
- **Unbounded job date:** the admin job date was unbounded and could mass-lapse the portfolio. Fixed by AC-07.8.
- **Dashboard 500:** an extreme dashboard `asOf` gave a 500. Fixed by AC-11.6.
- **Offer race:** a renewal offer could race a cancel. The offer now takes the policy lock, and `repository/CLAUDE.md`
  records the rule.
- **Test minors:** two mislabelled test names, and the CSS-class locator in the dashboard Playwright test.
