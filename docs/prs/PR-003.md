## Changes
- **Root `CLAUDE.md` rule 3:** `main` (production) and `dev` (integration) are both protected. Features are cut from
  `dev` and merged into `dev` through `/pr`. `dev` is promoted to `main` through `/release` after QA.
- **`no-direct-main-commit.sh` hook:**
  - Protects both `main` and `dev` against direct commits, rebases, history-moving resets and non-fast-forward pulls.
  - Checks merge sources: `dev` accepts `feat/`, `fix/` and `chore/`; `main` accepts `dev` and `hotfix/`. `--no-ff`
    is always required.
  - Allows pushing a protected branch under its own name. Blocks force pushes, deletes, `--mirror`, and pushing any
    other ref onto `main` or `dev`.
  - Fixes a latent bug: the text after `-m` was being counted as a merge source.
- **Regression suite:** `test_hooks.py` grows from 77 to 138 cases, all passing.
- **`/pr`** now targets `dev`. The new **`/release`** command runs full QA on `dev`, writes a release record, merges
  `--no-ff` into `main`, tags and pushes.
- Docs (`harness-setup.md`, `/new-product`, `docs/prs/README.md`) are updated. `.playwright-mcp/` is gitignored.
