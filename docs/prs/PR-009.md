## Scope
- **Quote ownership (AC-01.12, AC-01.13, AC-03.9).**
  - A signed-in customer's quote records them as owner (Flyway V7). Anonymous quotes have no owner and are claimed by
    the first customer who applies.
  - Applying for someone else's quote gets the same 400 `QUOTE_NOT_FOUND` as an unknown quote.
  - The owner is never returned by `GET /api/quotes/{id}`. This resolves S2 security minor 4.
- **Issuance (AC-05.1–05.4).**
  - `POST /api/applications/{id}/issue` turns an APPROVED application into an ACTIVE policy.
  - Numbers are `PF-<TL|MO|HH>-<year>-<6 digits>`, from a locked per-product, per-year sequence.
  - The effective date may be today up to today + 60 days, and the policy expires after its term.
  - The policy, its first version, first term and first transition are written in one transaction (V8).
  - `GET /api/policies/mine` and `GET /api/policies/{number}` responses are allow-listed: no health inputs and no
    KYC fields.
- **Endorsements (AC-06.1–06.7).**
  - Address change, add nominee (TERM_LIFE only, at most 3 nominees, shares up to 100%) and sum-insured change.
  - A sum-insured change re-rates on the policy's own rule version. For AC-06.2 the adjustment is
    (9490.00 − 6850.00) × 184 / 365 = 1330.85.
  - The endorsement, the new version and the ENDORSED transition are written atomically (V9). A concurrent write gets
    409 `POLICY_UPDATE_CONFLICT`.
  - Records are append-only, and no PUT, PATCH or DELETE endpoint exists.
  - **Preview endpoint (G-1)** runs the same checks and writes nothing.
- **State machine (AC-10.1, 10.2, 10.4; domain half of 10.3).**
  - The full 30-cell transition matrix is tested.
  - An invalid transition raises `InvalidPolicyStateException`, returned as 409 `/problems/invalid-policy-state`.
- **NFRs.** NFR-03.1 (log capture extended to issuance and endorsements), NFR-04.1, NFR-04.3 and NFR-04.4 (on every
  new route, including encoded paths).
- **Frontend.**
  - An Issue panel on Application Status.
  - My Policies, and Policy Detail with Versions, Endorsements, Status and Refunds tabs.
  - An Endorse page with a dynamic form; a sum-insured change must be previewed before Confirm.
  - Playwright journeys on desktop and mobile, with committed visual baselines.
- **Specs first:** `819a246` (quote ownership) and `0bdda51` (preview endpoint, G-1, approved at the checkpoint).
