- **For TASK-007 (E2E and the Evaluator walkthrough).** The seed now exists in every context, including the packaged app. On a fresh app customer1 sees S1 (TERM_LIFE, Renew panel at 6850.00, `payableUntil` = term end + 30), S2 and S3; customer2 sees S4, S5 (Renew, `payableUntil` = term end + 15) and S6, plus the REFERRED application. admin1's dashboard shows 2/2/2, 13700.00 / 30300.00 / 22700.00 (total 66700.00), S1 in the pipeline and S5 in the forecast. Seed names are "Seed Holder S1" to "S6", address "10 Seed Residency Road, Pune 411001".
- **Seed date arithmetic.** S1 and S5 are pinned by their term end through `plusMonths`/`minusMonths`, so in a leap-day edge case (T+21 on 29 February) the first term would end one day off the stated T+20. Nothing depends on it and the spec's AC-11.1 is unaffected.
- **A seed failure stops startup.** Like the U-14 seed, an exception in the portfolio seed propagates. Each policy is one transaction, so a failure leaves no half-made policy, and the next start retries the missing ones.
- **A forecast entry needs no offer.** `PortfolioRules.inLapseForecast` (TASK-001, P-11) lists any in-force policy whose current paid term ended before `asOf`, with or without an offer. This follows P-11 literally; a policy that never got an offer (SG-7) is in the forecast but never lapses.
- **`SG-6` as designed.** `premiumCollected` is not cut at `asOf`, so a historic `asOf` shows today's collected figure (the SPA can say so; the spec has no paid-date history).
- **Package-private `@Transactional`.** `issueBackdated` relies on Spring 6 applying transactions to package-visible methods of CGLIB proxies. It works under the test and packaged class loaders (the seed's transaction wraps it anyway, so atomicity does not depend on it).
