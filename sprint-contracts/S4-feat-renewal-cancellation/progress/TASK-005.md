- **V11 (C8).** `V11__refund_record.sql` adds `refund_record`: unique on `policy_number`, foreign key to `policy`, money as `NUMERIC(19,2)` (premium, fee, amount), no floating-point column, and CHECKs as a last line of defence (a refund is never negative and never above its premium basis; `1 <= unexpired_days <= term_days`). V1 to V9 are byte-identical to `12f95c3` and V10 is identical to its TASK-004 commit `249e684` (checked with `git hash-object`; `RefundRecordMigrationTest` also pins Flyway's checksum of V1 to V10).
- **Entity and repository.** `RefundRecordEntity` is `@Immutable`, built only through `of(...)`, no setters, `toString` shows policy number and term only. `RefundRecordRepository extends Repository` with `save`, `findByPolicyNumberOrderByIdAsc` and `findByPolicyNumberIn`. `AppendOnlyArchTest` gained three existence checks (entity, repository, set-wide coverage) that failed in the red commit while the classes were absent.
- **Domain.** `CancellationReason` (G-4): the four names, matched exactly. The calculator, date rule and premium basis are TASK-001's.
- **`CancellationService`.** One `prepare` shared by preview and cancel, so the preview shows exactly what the cancel writes. P-5 order. Cancel: body shape (400: unknown keys sanitised, a malformed date, a missing or unknown reason, all reported together), then 404, then 409 (the state machine refuses CANCELLED for LAPSED and CANCELLED), then 400 `CANCELLATION_DATE_OUT_OF_TERM` naming `cancellationDate`. Preview: `date` format (400), then 404, 409, then the window naming `date`. The term is the current paid term (G-5); the fee and cooling-off come from that term's own rule version; only term 1 has cooling-off, measured from term 1's start. The premium basis is the term premium plus the adjustments of the endorsements effective in that term (8180.85 in the AC example). `cancel` is one `@Transactional` method: refund insert (IDENTITY, so it reaches the database at once), policy to CANCELLED through the aggregate, transition row (reason = the request's reason, actor = the customer). A lost race (unique key, optimistic lock, lock timeout) is 409 `POLICY_UPDATE_CONFLICT`; a sequential second cancel is 409 invalid-policy-state. No audit entry and no new version (P-10).
- **Web.** `CancellationController`: `GET /api/policies/{n}/cancel/preview?date=` (C3, 200) and `POST /api/policies/{n}/cancel` (C4, `consumes` JSON only, 201 `{refund, policy}`), both `@PreAuthorize("hasRole('CUSTOMER')")`, one service call each. The body is captured (`reason`, `cancellationDate`, unknown keys), so a refund amount can never be supplied. `PolicyController.RefundResponse` is the C5 Refund; `refunds[]` in the detail is now filled in append order (`PolicyDetail.RefundView`, `PolicyQueryService`).
